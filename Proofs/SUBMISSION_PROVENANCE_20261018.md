It does not certify worldwide priority, journal publication, independent peer review, or repository admission.

The current Lean modules and their bibliographies were read for this inventory. The primary-source and proof reviews recorded in individual PRs remain separate evidence; copying a bibliography entry here does not mean every cited paper was freshly read in full. In particular, an upload date is not a publication date. Gebendorfer's recent manuscripts are author-supplied research manuscripts. Public reviewer comments, internal computational replay, and Lean kernel certificates have different scopes.

The public review checked stated finite and symbolic parts; it is not journal peer review or a complete Lean proof.
